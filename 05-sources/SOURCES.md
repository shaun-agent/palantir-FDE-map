## Note on the internal onboarding document

Palantir's internal onboarding document is **not public**. The only well-known Palantir leak is a
2013-era sales document about products and clients
([TechCrunch coverage](https://techcrunch.com/2015/01/11/leaked-palantir-doc-reveals-uses-specific-functions-and-key-clients/)),
not onboarding material. The closest public proxies for "how Palantir onboards FDEs" are the
Dev-versus-Delta post, Qureshi's essay (which names the onboarding reading list: *Impro*,
*The Looming Tower*, *Interviewing Users*, *Getting Things Done*), and the Foundry technical intro.
